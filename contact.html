Contact & Support

Updated November 19, 2026.

For help generating a label, follow the step-by-step guide and frequently asked questions.

If generation fails, check that your UPC has 11 digits or a valid 12-digit check digit. For CSV errors, verify the upc header and preserve leading zeros. For printing problems, use US Letter paper at 100% scale and test alignment on plain paper first.

For questions, bug reports or corrections, email hello@getupclabel.com. Include your browser, operating system, label template and a non-confidential example. Do not share confidential product data.

For number licensing or ownership questions, contact your local GS1 organisation. This tool cannot assign numbers or change retailer product records.
